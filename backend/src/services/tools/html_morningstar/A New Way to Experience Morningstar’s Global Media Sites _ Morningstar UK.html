A New Way to Experience Morningstar’s Global Media Sites

We’ve upgraded our global media sites with a more streamlined design.

Adley Bowden 28 Jun 2025

At Morningstar, we believe every investor deserves access to the best tools and insights so they can make decisions, no matter how the market moves. In service of that mission, we’ve upgraded our global media sites with a more streamlined design, reliable data, actionable research, and powerful tools. These changes go beyond just improving features; they build stronger connections with investors worldwide.

One connected experience

Go global or stay local. Our consolidated navigation makes it easy to access insights from across the world. You’ll get data and research tailored to your home market or closest relevant region, and when it’s time to expand the view of any given security or market trend, our media sites connect regionally focused news to global investing themes such as inflation, interest rates, and sustainability.

Deeper, more meaningful data

We’ve expanded the scope of our sector performance metrics to help you focus on the information you really need. Plus, our refreshed lists of top-rated investments—ranked using Morningstar’s trusted, objective analysis—can help you make informed decisions even faster than before.

Upgraded investment reports

Investment reports now load faster, are more visually dynamic, and easier to navigate—all without compromising Morningstar’s signature depth and authority. Refreshed visual designs and intuitive page layouts draw your focus to what matters most: ratings, risk, performance, and analyst commentary.

Enhanced editorial experience

Across all markets, you can rely on Morningstar’s deep, ongoing coverage of essential investing categories: markets, funds, ETFs, and stocks. We’re also increasing our coverage of key investor themes of bonds, personal finance, sustainability, retirement, and the economy. Plus, select Morningstar sites will receive timely, high-quality video content that helps you understand markets more quickly and effectively.

High-powered investment screening

Our new Screener experience isn’t just a cut-and-dry filter: It’s an interactive discovery engine. Use categorical filters tailored by investment type to quickly sort by key traits such as fund size, Morningstar category, sustainability score, and more. When it’s time to narrow down your winners, side-by-side comparison views help you seamlessly analyze fees, ratings, risk, and performance metrics.

Watchlists for all your investment ideas

Keep track of investment opportunities with a central list or create multiple lists to track different goals, regions, asset types, or strategies. And with new data views—such as fund data, performance, equity data, and more—your watchlists automatically adjust to the asset types you choose.

What’s next

In the coming months, we’ll launch our new free registered account, which will give you unlimited access to our trusted investment reports, Screener, and watchlists. You’ll also get access to premium fund ratings (a new feature for users in Asia and Canada) and receive exclusive newsletters and other insights via email.

We’re excited to offer investors like you the tools and insights you need to navigate an ever-changing world and welcome your feedback as we continue to evolve our site experience.
